under the hood

Three readings, one verdict.

Every submission passes through three separate readings before you see a number. Each one has a single job, so the final score is easy to trust and easy to check.

What happens when you click "Score this match"

1

The job description gets structured

The posting is read once and turned into a short profile: title, seniority, required skills, nice-to-have skills, minimum experience, and the core responsibilities — the same shorthand a recruiter would jot down after a first read.

2

The resume gets structured the same way

The candidate's resume is read and reduced to the facts that matter for matching: name, total years of experience, core skills, most recent role, and education.

3

The two profiles are compared

Skills, experience, and seniority are weighed against each other directly, and the result comes back as a score out of 100, a plain-language verdict, and the specific strengths and gaps that led to it.

What it's built on

automation

The three readings run as connected workflows, so a single submission triggers all of them in sequence automatically.

language model

Each reading is done by an AI model — the same one for all three steps, so the job description and resume are judged by consistent standards.

record keeping

Every parsed job description, parsed resume, and match score is logged to a spreadsheet, so nothing scored today is lost by tomorrow.

HireSmartly — built on n8n, Groq, and Google Sheets.
